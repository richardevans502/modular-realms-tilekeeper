## Reporting Issues
When reporting a bug, please include:
1. The app version (found in Settings > About).
2. Your device/OS version.
3. Steps to reproduce the issue.
4. Any error messages you see.

TileKeeper stores all data locally on your device, so we may ask you to export a diagnostic log from **Settings > Diagnostics** if you choose to share it.
